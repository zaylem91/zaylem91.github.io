Kuro Bot uses a powerful PostgreSQL database to securely store all your project data. This means your information is always safe, searchable, and organized — even if you're managing thousands of chapters.

Automatic backups to your Google Drive account ensure you never lose important files or project data. Set up once and never worry about data loss again.

Access everything through a clean, responsive web interface that works on desktop and mobile. Check progress, manage your team, and configure settings from anywhere.
